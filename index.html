About RAIVEGA

Multimodal Freight Forwarding with Reliable Service Discipline

RAIVEGA is built around shipment coordination that values planning, responsiveness and operational control. Our approach combines freight execution with practical customer support across ocean, air, ground, customs and warehousing requirements.

We work to simplify movement through clear communication, timely coordination and dependable service follow-through from origin to destination.

Founded with a focus on execution over promises, RAIVEGA has grown into a trusted multimodal freight partner for businesses across India and global trade lanes. We work directly with shippers, traders and procurement teams to keep cargo moving on schedule.

Our team combines deep logistics knowledge with hands-on coordination covering FCL/LCL ocean bookings, air uplift, domestic road and rail, customs brokerage, and bonded warehousing under one responsive team.

We are headquartered in Ludhiana, Punjab and serve clients across textiles, auto components, engineering goods, FMCG and project cargo sectors.
